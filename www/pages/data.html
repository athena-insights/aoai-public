Charts

Each chart on the Overall and Groups pages has two downloads to the right of its title. One saves the chart as an image. The other saves its data as a TSV, a plain text file that can be opened by spreadsheet applications. The TSV is organized as tidy data.

Pooled samples

As described on the Methodology page, we also pool two adjacent waves of the survey, which by design share no respondents, to increase precision. These pooled samples are available for analysis, and we provide their sizes and full-sample margins of error in the table below.
