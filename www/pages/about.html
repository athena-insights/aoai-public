Americans on AI is a project to understand Americans' evolving views on artificial intelligence.

The Americans on AI biweekly survey of US adults, launched in July 2026, is fielded by NORC at the University of Chicago.

AI is playing an increasingly large role in American life. One-off surveys, each with their own scope and methods, capture a single moment in time. With the technology, and people's views about it, changing rapidly, our tracking survey sets a high-quality baseline of Americans' attitudes and uncovers changes over time, for all adults and different demographic groups.

The survey questionnaire was co-developed by Athena Insights and Early Studies, based on qualitative interviews and more than 20 pilot studies. We continue to evolve the survey instrument and methodology, and welcome feedback.

We have several other studies underway, which will be published in the future.

The team behind Americans on AI is Caroline Behringer, president of Athena Insights, and Colin Bortner, its head of research.

Americans on AI is funded by individual donors who share our interest in giving the public and decision-makers high-quality public opinion data on AI. Our funders have no say in the project's design or implementation.

Athena Insights

Athena Insights is a US-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit working to democratize access to world-class narrative research. We deploy the same sophisticated market research methods that built global brands and make the insights available for free to those working for a better future.

More at athenainsights.org ↗.

Early Studies

Early Studies is a London-based signals intelligence company dedicated to understanding the complexities of human behavior, attitudes, and societal change. Using advanced research methodologies, they specialize in understanding how beliefs shape reality.

More at earlystudies.com ↗.
